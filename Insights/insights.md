# Level 2 Task 1 – Key Insights

## 1. Table Booking

Out of 9,551 restaurants:

- 1,158 restaurants (12.12%) offer table booking.
- 8,393 restaurants (87.88%) do not offer table booking.

Table booking is therefore available at a relatively small proportion of restaurants in the dataset.

---

## 2. Online Delivery

Out of 9,551 restaurants:

- 2,451 restaurants (25.66%) offer online delivery.
- 7,100 restaurants (74.34%) do not offer online delivery.

Online delivery is more common than table booking, but it is still unavailable at most restaurants.

---

## 3. Table Booking and Restaurant Ratings

The average aggregate rating is:

| Table Booking | Average Rating |
|---|---:|
| Yes | 3.44 |
| No | 2.56 |

Restaurants offering table booking have an average rating 0.88 points higher than restaurants without table booking.

This is an observed relationship in the dataset and does not establish that table booking causes higher ratings.

---

## 4. Online Delivery by Price Range

Online delivery availability varies across price ranges:

| Price Range | Online Delivery |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 15.77% |
| 2 | 41.31% |
| 3 | 29.19% |
| 4 | 9.04% |

Price Range 2 has the highest proportion of restaurants offering online delivery at 41.31%.

Price Range 4 has the lowest proportion at 9.04%.

---

## 5. Overall Conclusion

The analysis shows that table booking and online delivery are not available at most restaurants in the dataset.

Restaurants with table booking have a noticeably higher average aggregate rating than restaurants without table booking.

Among the four price ranges, Price Range 2 shows the highest online-delivery availability, while Price Range 4 shows the lowest.

These findings are descriptive and represent patterns observed in the provided dataset.
